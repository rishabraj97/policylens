PolicyLens Compliance Test Policy

1. Data Protection & Privacy Notice
Organizations processing personal data must maintain an updated privacy notice published on the public website.
The privacy notice must be reviewed and re-approved annually by the Legal & Compliance Department.

2. Access Control & Encryption
The IT Security Department must implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all administrative portals by Q4 2026.
All customer transaction records and personal identifiable information must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

3. Incident Reporting
Security breaches involving customer data must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 72 hours of identification.
The Incident Response Team must prepare a formal post-incident remediation report.

4. Data Retention
Financial audit logs and transaction records must be retained securely for a minimum of 7 years.
Evidence of regular data purge audits must be submitted to the Compliance Officer semi-annually.
